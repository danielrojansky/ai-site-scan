What counts as one report

One report is one site, by host name, such as example.com. www.example.com is the same site. blog.example.com is a different site. Unused Pro reports do not carry over to the next month.

How you get it

  1. Check your site for free.
  2. Log in with your email (we send you a 6-digit code) or with Google. There is no password.
  3. Choose a plan and pay on Polar's secure checkout.
  4. You come back to AI Site Scan, and every fix opens right away. We email you the report and save it in your account. Log in later to open it again.

Delivery is instant and digital. Nothing is shipped. See Refunds and delivery.

Pro renews every month

Pro is a subscription. Polar charges $29 every month on the same day until you cancel. Cancel anytime in your account under "Receipts and subscription". After you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the month you paid for, and nothing more is charged.

Payment and tax

Checkout runs on Polar (polar.sh), our merchant of record. Polar is the seller on your receipt. It takes the payment and adds sales tax or VAT where the law requires it. You see the final price before you pay. We never see or store your card details.

Refunds

Full report: email support@getaisitescan.com within 7 days of your purchase for a full refund, no reason needed. Pro: see the refund policy.

Questions

Why do I need to log in?

So we can email you the report and keep it in your account. You log in with a one-time code or with Google. There is no password to remember.

Can I use the report for a client's site?

Yes. Check the client's site, unlock its report, and use the guide and its code on that site.

Do you make the changes for me?

No. The report is a self-serve digital product. You, your developer or your AI coding tool make the changes. We never log in to your site.

Which payment methods can I use?

Cards, and the other methods that Polar's checkout shows for your country.
