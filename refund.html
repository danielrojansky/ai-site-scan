Refunds, cancellation and delivery

Updated November 27, 2026 · by Daniel Rojansky

  • One-time payment. No subscription. We never charge you again.
  • Instant digital delivery. Nothing is shipped.
  • Full refund within 7 days, no reason needed.

What you buy

A digital product: the full fix guide for 1 site ($19) or for 10 sites ($49). A license key unlocks it on AI Site Scan. The guide lists every fix for the site, with steps and ready-to-paste code, and you can download it as one Markdown file.

Delivery

  1. You pay on Polar's secure checkout.
  2. Polar sends you back to AI Site Scan. Your license key is applied, and every fix opens on screen within seconds.
  3. Polar emails you a receipt. It has a link to your Polar customer portal, where your license key is kept.
  4. Use the key to unlock the same site in another browser, or, with the 10-site pack, to unlock more sites.

If you do not get your key within 15 minutes, check your spam folder, then email support@getaisitescan.com. We will send it to you.

Cancellation

There is no subscription, so there is nothing to cancel. If you bought by mistake, ask for a refund.

Refunds

  • Email support@getaisitescan.com within 7 days of your purchase. Write from the email you used at checkout, or include it and the order number.
  • You get a full refund. You do not need to give a reason.
  • We refund through Polar to the payment method you used. Your bank may need 5 to 10 business days to show it.
  • After a refund, the license key stops working.
  • After 7 days, we still refund you if we could not deliver the guide, or if it does not work because of a fault on our side.

Before you dispute a charge

Please email us first. We answer fast, and a refund from us is quicker than a dispute with your bank.

Price changes

We may change prices for future orders. A change never affects an order you already paid.
