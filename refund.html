Refunds, cancellation and delivery

Updated November 28, 2026 · by Daniel Rojansky

  • Instant digital delivery. The report opens on screen, arrives by email and stays in your account. Nothing is shipped.
  • Full report ($19): one-time payment. Full refund within 7 days, no reason needed.
  • Pro ($29 a month): cancel anytime. It stays active until the end of the paid month.

What you buy

A digital product: the full AI readiness report for a site, with every fix, steps and ready-to-paste code, as one Markdown file. The Full report covers 1 site and 1 rescan within 30 days. Pro covers 10 reports a month, with rescans of those sites during the month.

Delivery

  1. You log in with your email or Google, then pay on Polar's secure checkout.
  2. Polar sends you back to AI Site Scan, and every fix opens on screen within seconds.
  3. We email you the report and save it in your account. Log in at any time to open it again.
  4. Polar emails you a receipt.

If the report does not open or does not arrive within 15 minutes, check your spam folder, then email support@getaisitescan.com. We will fix it.

Cancel Pro

Log in, open "My reports", and click "Receipts and subscription". There you can cancel in a few clicks. You can also email us and we will cancel it for you. After you cancel, nothing more is charged, and Pro stays active until the end of the month you paid for.

Refunds

  • Full report: email support@getaisitescan.com within 7 days of your purchase. You get a full refund, and you do not need to give a reason.
  • Pro: if you did not use any report in a month, email us within 7 days of that month's charge and we refund it. If you forgot to cancel, tell us: we will be fair.
  • Write from the email you log in with, or include it and the order number.
  • We refund through Polar to the payment method you used. Your bank may need 5 to 10 business days to show it.
  • After a refund, the reports from that payment are no longer available.
  • After 7 days, we still refund you if we could not deliver the report, or if it does not work because of a fault on our side.

Before you dispute a charge

Please email us first. We answer fast, and a refund from us is quicker than a dispute with your bank.

Price changes

We may change prices for future orders. For Pro, we email you at least 30 days before a new price applies, and you can cancel before then.
