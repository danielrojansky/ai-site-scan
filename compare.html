AI Site Scan vs other AI visibility checkers

Updated November 28, 2026 · by Daniel Rojansky

Most AI visibility tools tell you what is wrong. AI Site Scan also proves it with real bot requests, shows you the page an AI tool actually reads, and gives you the code to fix it, filled in for your site.

  • Free check, no login: score, every issue and the top 3 fixes with code.
  • Real tests: 14 AI crawlers and assistants request your page, and we show what your server answered.
  • Proof you can see: your page without JavaScript, the way AI tools read it, next to a browser screenshot.
  • Fixes you can paste: JSON-LD, llms.txt, robots.txt and head tags, written for your pages.
  • Fair price: $19 once for 1 site with 1 rescan, or $29 a month for 10 reports. No yearly plan.

Why sites pick AI Site Scan

It tests, not guesses

Reading robots.txt tells you what a bot is allowed to do. Our scan sends requests as 14 AI bots and records what your server and firewall really answered. A CDN or bot shield that blocks AI is visible here.

You see what AI sees

Many sites look full in a browser but are empty without JavaScript. We put the AI view next to a screenshot, so you see the gap in seconds.

Code, not homework

Other tools give tips or a prompt. The full report gives ready-to-paste code with your business name, pages and details already in it. You, your developer or your AI coding tool paste it in.

Pay once, not every month

Monitoring tools charge every month. If you need the fixes for one site, you pay $19 once, then rescan to confirm. Agencies use Pro for 10 sites a month.

When another tool is a better fit

  • You want to track how ChatGPT and other AI answers mention your brand, week after week: Otterly.AI is built for that.
  • You want a one-time view of your brand's sentiment and share of voice in AI answers: HubSpot AI Search Grader does that for free.
  • Use AI Site Scan first to make sure AI tools can reach and read your site. Brand monitoring only helps after that.
