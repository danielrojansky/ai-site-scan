# AI Site Scan: see your website the way AI sees it

> Free check of how ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI reach, read and understand a website, and whether AI agents can use it. URL: https://getaisitescan.com/

Most AI tools read your raw HTML and skip what scripts draw. AI Site Scan tests a page the way they do and shows what they miss: your name, your address, your prices.

## What a free scan shows

- A score from 0 to 100 in 9 areas (robots.txt, structured data, content, brand signals and more), and a score for ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.
- Get in: 14 AI crawlers and assistants request the page, from GPTBot and ClaudeBot to PerplexityBot.
- Read: the page with JavaScript off, next to a real browser screenshot.
- Understand and trust: structured facts (JSON-LD), contact details, dates and official profiles.
- Find more: the sitemap, llms.txt and 23 agent-readiness standards, rated as a level from 0 to 5.
- Use: the page in a real browser, the way ChatGPT agent, Claude in Chrome and Gemini see it, with an agent grade from A to F.

## Three kinds of AI visit a site

1. Search crawlers (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) get the raw HTML. Many do not run JavaScript.
2. Agents that read the page (Claude in Chrome, Perplexity Comet) use the accessibility tree: buttons, links and fields by name.
3. Agents that look at the screen (ChatGPT agent, Gemini in Chrome) use screenshots, so pop-ups over the page block them.
