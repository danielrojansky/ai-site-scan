Privacy policy

Updated November 28, 2026 · by Daniel Rojansky

Who is responsible

Daniel Rojansky, trading as Daniel Consulting, Israel, is responsible for your data. Contact: support@getaisitescan.com.

What we collect and why

  • Addresses you check. We fetch the page you enter, and some public files of that site, to make your report. The result stays in a server cache for 10 minutes. Server logs keep the site, the score and the time for a short time. For the line "better than N% of sites", we keep the site address, the date and the score for 90 days. This is not linked to you.
  • Scan history. It stays only in your browser. You can delete it in the History tab.
  • Your account. Your email address. If you log in with Google, also your name and profile picture from Google. We use them to log you in and to send your reports.
  • Your reports and purchases. The reports you unlock (site, score, fixes), what you bought and how many reports you have left. They are saved in your account so you can open them again.
  • Emails. We email you your reports and, if you ask for it on the free check, one reminder. Emails go out through Resend. We do not add you to a newsletter.
  • Messages you send us. We keep them to answer you and to keep a record of support requests.
  • Analytics. PostHog records page views, clicks and scan steps, so we can improve the product. Until you accept cookies, it stores nothing in your browser. If you accept, it keeps an ID in a cookie and in local storage. Session recordings hide what you type.
  • Advertising. Only if you accept cookies, Google Ads measures whether a visit from an ad led to a purchase.
  • Payments. Polar processes payments as our merchant of record. We receive your name, email, country and what you bought. We never see your card details.

Legal bases

We use your data to give you what you asked for, such as an account, a report or a purchase (contract). We use analytics and advertising cookies, and send a reminder email, only with your consent. We keep short server logs to protect the service from abuse (legitimate interest).

Who processes it

These service providers process data for us, each under its own data protection terms: Vercel (hosting), Supabase (login and database), PostHog (analytics), Resend (email), Polar (payments, as merchant of record), Google (login, only if you choose it, and ads, only with your consent). They may process data in the United States, the European Union and other countries. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for other companies' marketing.

How long we keep it

  • Scan results: 10 minutes in the server cache.
  • Site address, date and score for the comparison: 90 days.
  • Server logs: a few days at most.
  • Your account and saved reports: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Support messages: up to 2 years.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires. Polar keeps the payment records as merchant of record.

Cookies and browser storage

Always on, because the site needs them: your login session, your scan history and your cookie choice. They stay in your browser's local storage.

Only after you accept: PostHog analytics (a cookie and a local storage ID) and Google Ads conversion cookies. To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser and choose again.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it (including your whole account), or object to how we use it. You can withdraw your consent at any time. Email support@getaisitescan.com, and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Israel, that is the Privacy Protection Authority.

Children

AI Site Scan is for website owners and businesses. It is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

The whole site uses HTTPS. Logins use one-time codes or Google, so we store no passwords. Card details go only to Polar's checkout. We keep as little data as we can.

Changes

We post every change on this page and update the date at the top.
