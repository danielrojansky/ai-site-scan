About AI Site Scan

Updated November 28, 2026 · by Daniel Rojansky

AI Site Scan shows how AI tools see a website. You enter an address, and in 1 to 3 seconds you get an AI readiness score from 0 to 100, the page as an AI tool reads it, a test of 14 AI bots, and a ranked list of fixes.

Who makes it

AI Site Scan is made and run by Daniel Rojansky, an independent product and technology consultant in Israel (trade name: Daniel Consulting). It is a small, independent product. See the contact page for business details.

How the score works

  • The score covers 9 areas, such as robots.txt, structured data, content and brand signals, for a total of 100 points.
  • AI Site Scan adds its own checks: a test of 14 AI crawlers and assistants, 23 agent-readiness standards, page speed and size, headings, links and a location check for local businesses. These extra checks do not change the score.
  • The checks are rules in code. The fix guide is built from your results by the same rules. No AI model writes it.
  • A higher score means AI tools find the page easier to read. It does not promise citations or rankings.

How we make money

The check is free. We sell the full report in two ways: $19 once for 1 site, or Pro at $29 a month for 10 reports a month. There are no ads on the site, and we do not sell data. See Pricing.

What the scan keeps

Your scan history stays in your own browser. Our server keeps a result for 10 minutes so a repeat check is fast, and short-term logs. To compare sites, we keep only the site address, the date and the score for 90 days. See the privacy policy for details.
